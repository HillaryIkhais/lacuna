Every city has a history. But what happens when its people can no longer remember it?

Here, even the past has a price.

Somewhere in this city, someone is borrowing against its future. And the truth is buried in the memories people have already surrendered.

Your memories can buy you time. But the lender only sees what they can be sold for.

But a memory is more than collateral. Your mother's voice can reveal a hidden truth. Your childhood home can uncover something the city has buried. The face you remember can open doors that money alone cannot.

Some things are worth more while you still have them.

And time is not on your side. Every cycle adds to what you owe.

Still, the city has not forgotten everything. Every clue brings you closer to the truth. Every memory you keep may reveal something the others cannot.

You can settle the debt. You can reclaim what you pledged. But the choices you made have already shaped the journey.

Because the question was never just what your memories were worth.

It was what you were willing to lose to discover the truth.

LACUNA. The price of remembering.
